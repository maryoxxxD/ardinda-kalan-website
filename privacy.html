Privacy Policy

Effective: September 17, 2026

Purpose and scope

This policy describes how Ardında Kalan handles information when its private desktop publishing tool connects to TikTok. The tool is used by Ardında Kalan to publish its own short documentary content to its own social media account. It is not a public application.

Information used for the integration

When the TikTok connection is authorized, TikTok may provide the account information and permissions required for the publishing integration. This may include basic account identification, the permissions granted to the application, and authorization tokens needed to perform approved publishing actions.

Ardında Kalan uses this information only to connect the authorized account, verify publishing capability, and publish content when an authorized operator chooses to do so.

Passwords

Ardında Kalan does not collect or store TikTok passwords. TikTok authentication and consent take place through TikTok's own authorization process.

Storage and sharing

Authorization credentials used by the private publishing tool are stored locally with operating-system protection. Ardında Kalan does not sell user information. Information is not shared with third parties except as needed to operate the authorized integration with the relevant platform.

Control and revocation

The connected TikTok account can revoke the application's access through TikTok's account or authorization settings. Revocation prevents the tool from using that authorization for future publishing actions.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the publishing integration changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent through the official Ardında Kalan social media account from which this site was referenced.
