Ardında Kalan is an independent media project that produces short historical and documentary videos about places, people, and events worth remembering.

Private publishing tool

Our internal desktop tool is used only to publish Ardında Kalan content to the project's own social media accounts.

Publishing integration

The publishing tool helps the Ardında Kalan team prepare and send its own short documentary videos to its official channels. It is not offered as a public service and does not publish content for third parties.
